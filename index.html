FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the application download games?

Yes. Game pages in the Xbox and Epic catalogs allow you to start downloading supported games. You can also manage and launch games already available in your local library.

Is it free?

Yes, downloading and using the application is completely free.

Why does Windows display a SmartScreen warning?

Installers that are not signed with a paid code-signing certificate may be flagged by SmartScreen. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

My trailers won't play. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure the Microsoft WebView2 Runtime is installed. It is included with Windows 11 and available for free from Microsoft for Windows 10.

Where are my settings stored?

In %APPDATA%\UniversalDownloader on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

How do I update the application?

Download the latest version from this page and run the installer again.
